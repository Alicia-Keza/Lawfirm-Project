Our Background

Built on Expertise & Integrity

Rutabanks Notary and Legal Services Ltd is a registered private company limited by shares in Rwanda, established to provide professional, reliable, and accessible legal and notarial services to individuals, businesses, and institutions.

The firm is led by a qualified legal professional who holds a Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB) and a Diploma in Legal Practice from the Institute of Legal Practice and Development (ILPD). With hands-on experience in legal drafting, advisory work, and notarial services, our practice is grounded in both academic rigor and practical expertise.

We pride ourselves on maintaining the highest standards of professional conduct, ensuring that every client receives accurate, timely, and legally sound services tailored to their specific circumstances. Our approach is always client-centered — we listen carefully, advise clearly, and act decisively on your behalf.
